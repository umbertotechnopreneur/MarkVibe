MarkVibe reader theme assets
===========================

Snapshot: Markdown Preview Enhanced 0.8.40, Crossnote preview_theme and
prism_theme CSS. Local modifications: preview font imports removed. The
MarkVibe runtime scopes selectors, drops site chrome and external resources,
preserves document typography, and adapts highlight.js classes to Prism tokens.
